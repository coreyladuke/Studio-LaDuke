# Studio LaDuke House — APPROVED ART DIRECTION
**Approved November 23, 2026. Owner choices: `1C, 2C, 3C, 4C, 5C, 6C, 7C, 8C, 9B, 10C, 11C, 12B`.**

This document supersedes earlier assumptions that the House should be a restrained, expensive-looking rustic cabin. It does **not** repeal the owner's requirement for high-quality, immersive, physically believable environments or the existing release blockers in `HOUSE_VISUAL_BLOCKERS.md`.

## North star
**A beautifully cared-for, joyously strange artist's home in rainy Southeast Alaska, grown over decades of stories and collecting.** An unexpected room may be glorious, funny, or a little surreal; the house as a whole should still feel inhabited and architecturally coherent. Original art and personal history provide the soul. Excitement comes from the rooms themselves, not explanatory popups.

The house must **not** feel run-down, neglected, creepy, generic, uniformly brown, or like a luxury cabin showroom. It should feel curious, colorful, warm, eccentric, inviting, handcrafted, and **wild but livable** (9B), without slipping into random visual noise.

## Color and material grammar
Palette ingredients, not a paint bucket dumped equally into every room:

- **Architectural constants:** well-maintained dark/warm cedar and walnut, aged brass or chrome details, inky black, warm cream, high-contrast black-and-white checker patterns.
- **High-energy heroes:** persimmon orange `#f16627`; electric plum/purple `#6636a8`; deep aubergine `#34204d`; turquoise `#1cb5b3`; sunflower yellow `#ffc42b`; cranberry `#d64d62`.
- **Quiet bridges:** moss/green shag `#5d7351`, dark forest `#20372e`, smoke/charcoal `#242329`, warm plaster `#edd9b7`.
- **Materials:** rolled/tufted vinyl (including the owner's turquoise glitter Kustom cabinet reference), velvet, vintage laminate, chrome feet, richly worked hardwood, colored and leaded glass, stained plywood, real art papers and upholstery remnants.
- **Checkerboard:** black-white tiles at main transition moments; alternate scales on textiles, trims or cabinet doors. Do not put the same checkers on every single surface or flatten the world into graphic art.
- **Finish rule:** clean, intentional, handsome craftsmanship. Worn-in charm is okay; dirt, rot, sagging architecture and visual neglect are not.

## Architectural story and pacing
- **Entrance / front hall:** intriguing but credible, warm carved wood and a controlled checkerboard arrival. The real five-note-star stained glass is seen in the architecture and becomes a visual anchor.
- **Ground floor:** the color blooms. The living room's **orange-and-purple vinyl modular conversation setting** is the first "you've got to see this" moment. More cabinetry, colorful textiles and charming surprises as you approach the studio.
- **Upper floor:** personal creative artifacts; the music room mixes green shag, guitars, and incredible vintage gear. Bookshelves, patterned staircases, attic secrets.
- **Attic:** Maverick's distinct imaginative kingdom, fully custom, legible and physically reachable.
- **Basement:** the colorful weirdness peaks in the arcade, recording booth, intimate theater and puzzle rooms. Every physical artifact must do something.
- **Exterior:** rain-soaked Alaska woods, cedar architecture and a whimsical greenhouse; visual/weather continuity with the House.

## Critical authentic-asset requirements
The **actual five-note-star**, **actual yellow Tom DeLonge Strat bought at sixteen**, **actual pink guitar**, **real original artworks, paddles, textiles, designs and photographs** must be traced to reference material and represented accurately. Do not replace these with generic stock illustrations, invented emblems or recognizable unlicensed movie props. Refer to movie memories through **original transformative details**, rather than duplicating protected sets or copyrighted imagery.

## Motion, sound and discovery rules
Visible hinged doors swing; drawers slide; old switches click; glass reflects changing light; projectors actually project; cabinets play games; books reveal content; instruments link to music or respond musically. The room must remain beautiful with motion disabled. Avoid endless looping effects, unrelated glows and repetitive text boxes. All audio is opt-in and user-controlled.

**Mobile-first camera:** iPhone view should show a satisfying composition immediately. Users can drag to reveal the full room. Every hotspot maps to the visible object across pan/zoom and has a keyboard/focus equivalent. No decorative interactive rectangles.

## One-level-down visual acceptance
For **every** room: (1) unique architecture, (2) one palette/floor hero, (3) believable furniture, (4) one real tactile interaction, (5) physically coherent exits, (6) authentic visual references where appropriate, (7) a natural reason to continue exploring. Capture before/after center, left and right screenshots, plus mobile/desktop behavior, then mark visual sign-off.

## What this approval is **not**
This is approval of **direction**, not sign-off on any unfinished image, prop or room. Self-hosted Pexels photographs in `assets/house-plates/` are **temporary scaffolding**. The `HOUSE_SIGNOFF.md` visual/final checks remain unchecked until rooms are genuinely rebuilt and inspected.
