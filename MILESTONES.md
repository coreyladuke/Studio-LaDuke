# Studio LaDuke milestones

## Active milestone — Front Stoop dimensional environment

Use the approved photographic Front Hall and Main Hall as the material and lighting benchmark. Compare the current Front Stoop with the baseline visual audit and keep the working route through the door, halls, stairs, and return available.

| Gate | Acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Scene coverage | Eight horizontal and two vertical views show a continuous porch, façade, forest, ground and overhead environment. No plane edges or uncovered background. |
| Architectural fidelity | Door, frame, hardware, siding, porch floor and rails use consistent proportions, shadows and material scale. |
| Spatial movement | Near railing and forest layers show different relative motion. Door alignment, collision, touch look and walk work against visible geometry. |
| Performance | Compare frame pacing with the recorded baseline on the same setup. Verify a physical Chromebook and iPhone Safari before release. |
| Sign-off | Inspect before and after screenshots and both walkthroughs. Record evidence and explicit remaining gaps before marking B, T, V or S. |

Work on this milestone in isolation. Keep the approved photographic route and the Greenhouse V3 work available. Expand dimensional work to other views only after the Stoop meets the visual gate.

## Review protocol

Follow `HOUSE_MASTER_COMPLETION_PROMPT.md`, `HOUSE_APPROVED_ART_DIRECTION.md` and `HOUSE_SIGNOFF.md`. State the scope and acceptance criteria, capture a baseline, implement and verify, inspect the actual output, and repeat until the evidence supports review. Record progress, branch, commit hash and device limitations in `STATUS.md`. Keep production changes subject to explicit approval.
