- **4C, living room:** A **semicircular/caterpillar-shaped orange-and-purple vinyl modular sofa**, gathered around an extraordinary vintage coffee table. Real upholstery depth, shadows, and accurate perspective; no 2D sticker pasted on photo.

**Implementation order:** Develop and test the optional discovery thread and mixed-media journal, then design the common atrium and curved couch as spatially unified scenes. Commit quality improvements to live, without declaring unfinished visual rooms complete.

## Batch 02: Adventure through the artist's imagination

**1. How should the adventure unfold?**
- **1A:** Free exploration, no bigger quest. Every room stands alone.
- **1B:** Hidden threads connect objects into a secret story and final reveal, with no required order.
- **1C:** Chapter-based rooms that gradually unlock; more guided.
- **1D:** Free wandering for everyone **plus** an optional hidden thread across multiple rooms. **[APPROVED]**

**2. What should visitors be able to collect?**
- **2A:** Nothing tracked; all discoveries are physical and temporary.
- **2B:** A vintage ticket-style pocketbook with little discovered memories.
- **2C:** Audio/visual fragments, such as original recordings and objects, but no progress counter.
- **2D:** Mixed-media curiosities: ticket scraps, drawings, riffs and interactive objects, each tied to where it came from. **[APPROVED]**

Current nonintrusive implementation: an optional on-device **Pocketbook** proving that discoveries can carry between rooms. The look, wording and collection rules may change based on this answer. No visitor account, no analytics, no private information.

**3. Where does the signature five-music-note stained-glass star belong?**
- **3A:** Above the front door in an ornate transom.
- **3B:** Suspended above the central staircase so light falls across checkered steps.
- **3C:** In a tall interior arch connecting the entrance to the living room.
- **3D:** Skylight above the house's central atrium, with colors changing by time of day. **[APPROVED]**

Use ONLY the owner's **real five-music-note** silhouette/reference. Don't invent or substitute a normal star.

**4. What shape is the orange/purple modular vinyl couch?**
- **4A:** Sunken circular 1970s conversation pit with a built-in sofa.
- **4B:** Chunky, generous L-shaped modular, bright orange bases and purple bolsters.
- **4C:** Curving semicircle/caterpillar-style sofa around a wild vintage coffee table. **[APPROVED]**
- **4D:** Asymmetric collection of modular blocks, with contrasting orange and purple pieces that can be rearranged.
