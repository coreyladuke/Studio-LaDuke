Eight Ball

This is local two-player, pass-the-phone pool. The table stays open after the break. The first legally pocketed solid or stripe afterward assigns groups.

  • Hit one of your own balls first once groups are assigned.
  • After contact, pocket a ball or drive any ball to a rail.
  • A scratch or other foul gives the other player ball in hand.
  • After clearing your group, pocket the 8 ball legally to win.
  • Pocketing the 8 early, or scratching while pocketing it, loses the rack.

Controls: drag around the cue ball to rotate the cue. Pull the left meter downward. Releasing the meter shoots.
